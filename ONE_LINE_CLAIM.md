## 4. Claim Validation Test

- **Test 1: Could another generic frontend developer use the same claim?**
  - *No*. Generic frontend developers focus on landing layouts, standard CRUD structures, or general animation libraries. This claim requires deep knowledge of LLM streaming states, citation drawer rendering, and RAG tracking logs.
- **Test 2: Does HIREVIUM provide evidence for it?**
  - *Yes*. The recruiter dashboards and interview evaluation matrices display dynamic candidate matching scores transparently.
- **Test 3: Does INDRA AI provide evidence for it?**
  - *Yes*. Pairing grounded RAG answers with trust badges and side drawer citation drawers directly makes LLM outputs transparent.
- **Test 4: Does StackScout provide evidence for it?**
  - *Yes*. The agent crawl logs and comparison matrices make autonomous web crawling and pricing analysis observable.
- **Test 5: Would an AI/ML startup hiring manager understand what I can do?**
  - *Yes*. They immediately recognize that this candidate can design the client-side screens needed to make their LLM backend models understandable and trusted by real users.
- **Test 6: Does the claim avoid pretending to be a senior ML researcher or DevOps engineer?**
  - *Yes*. It positions Aditya as a B.Tech student who specializes in **UI/UX design and React integration**, not model pre-training or infrastructure scaling.
