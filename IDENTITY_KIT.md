# Aditya Srivastav — Identity Kit

This document establishes the visual identity system for the personal portfolio of Aditya Srivastav, Frontend AI Engineer.

---

## 1. Positioning
- **Role**: Frontend AI Engineer
- **Target Audience**: Hiring Managers and Tech Leads at AI/ML-first startups.
- **Visual Direction**: Calm, precise, and human-made. The branding remains quiet so that the engineering work and projects remain the primary visual focus.

---

## 2. Typography

| Role | Font Family | Selection Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Headings** | **Space Grotesk** | A highly structured, geometric sans-serif that communicates technical precision, structural clarity, and a modern engineering focus. |
| **Body** | **Inter** | An exceptionally clean, highly readable neutral sans-serif that ensures long-form readability across case studies and resume sections. |

---

## 3. Color Palette

A restrained, high-contrast light palette designed to keep readability high and eliminate generic dark-cyberpunk clichés.

| Token | Color | HEX | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary** | Steel Slate | `#334155` | Subheadings, cards, structural borders, and icons. |
| **Text** | Slate Navy | `#0f172a` | Headers, paragraphs, body text, and high-emphasis elements. |
| **Background** | Soft Paper | `#fcfcfd` | Main page background and clean panels. |
| **Accent** | Tech Cobalt | `#2563eb` | Primary call-to-actions, active navigation highlights, and interactive states. |

---

## 4. Logo & Favicon

### Logo Concept
A minimal, geometric typographic monogram based on the letters **AS**. It encloses the initials inside a clean, thin-bordered technical frame, indicating structured engineering and attention to detail.

### Logo Asset
Located at `/logo.svg`. Renders the typographic monogram in high-contrast Slate Navy (`#0f172a`).

### Favicon Asset
Located at `/favicon.svg`. A square adaptation of the monogram optimized for small-scale browser tab visibility.

---

## 5. Mood
**Calm Technical** — A precise, clean, and confident visual direction. By avoiding neon gradients and floating 3D robots, the interface presents itself as a tool built by a thoughtful engineer who values user experience, semantic code, and practical product applications.

---
