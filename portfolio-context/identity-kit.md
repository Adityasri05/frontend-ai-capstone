## 2. Typography System
- **Headings**: Space Grotesk (`700 Bold`, `600 SemiBold`, `500 Medium`) — geometric, structured sans-serif communicating engineering precision.
- **Body & UI**: Inter (`400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`) — clean, highly readable neutral sans-serif for UI screens and long-form case studies.
- **Code & Metadata**: Monospace font (`font-mono`).

---

## 3. Color Palette & Tokens
- **Slate Navy (`#0f172a`)**: Primary text, headings, prominent borders (15.8:1 WCAG AAA contrast).
- **Soft Paper (`#fcfcfd`)**: Near-white light background and clean panels.
- **Steel Slate (`#334155`)**: Secondary text, badge outlines, and structural cards (8.2:1 contrast).
- **Tech Cobalt (`#2563eb`)**: Primary brand accent, CTA buttons, and active link states (4.6:1 contrast).
- **Slate Border (`#e2e8f0`)**: Subtle hairline layout borders.
