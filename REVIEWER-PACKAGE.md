## 4. In-Depth Evaluation Questions

After answering the two initial questions, please explore the homepage, project cards, case studies, and contact form, then answer the following:

### A. Clarity
1. What do you think this person does day-to-day?
2. What specific job or role would you associate with this portfolio? (e.g., Junior Frontend AI Engineer, Full-Stack Dev, etc.)
3. Was any headline or description confusing or overloaded with jargon?

### B. Proof & Technical Evidence
1. Which project made you believe this person can actually build AI-integrated products? (*HIREVIUM*, *INDRA AI*, *StackScout*, or *ResQra*?)
2. Which project felt least convincing or needed more technical detail?
3. Did the project descriptions support the claim made in the hero section?

### C. Navigation & Layout
1. Was it immediately obvious where to look next?
2. Was anything difficult to find on mobile or desktop?
3. Did you understand what each project was about from the summary card?

### D. Trust & Credibility
1. Did anything make the portfolio feel unfinished or generic?
2. Did any claim feel exaggerated or lack concrete proof?
3. Did anything make you question whether the projects were real?

### E. Primary Call to Action (CTA)
1. Did you know what primary action the portfolio wanted you to take?
2. Is the LinkedIn contact channel / Contact Form easy to find and use?
3. If you were a hiring manager at an AI startup, would you reach out?

### F. Overall Feedback
1. What is the single **strongest** part of the portfolio?
2. What is the single **weakest** part of the portfolio?
3. If you could change **only one thing**, what would it be?
